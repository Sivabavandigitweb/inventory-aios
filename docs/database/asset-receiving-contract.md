## Receiving and procurement

**Confirmed:** record arrival immediately; capture received date, receiver, quantity, location and specifications; documents are optional (R-01/R-03). Maintain purchase and warranty information (R-02); missing-information reminders are required capabilities (R-12). The PDF does not define units, price conventions or exception/completeness rules.

**Proposed header:** one Receipt represents an actual arrival at one receiving location and received_at, with received_by_id pointing to the physical receiver under the approved identity policy. Authenticated recorder identity remains separate in transaction audit/provenance. Do not default a receiver or a historical received time to the current user/time. If goods arrive at different locations, use separate linked arrivals rather than pretend a single header location describes both. Changes of storage location after receipt are transfers, not edits to original receipt location. Existing Receipt metadata is unchanged; non-user receivers would require a separately reviewed reference design (ABD-03).

**Proposed line:** id UUID; receipt_id/product_id FKs; positive line_number unique within the receipt; quantity_received; a stable approved unit reference; supplier_id; purchase_date; purchase_reference; unit_cost/currency; warranty_start/end/terms; received_specifications; timestamps. Specific numeric scale, required/nullable procurement values and representation of unknown/not-applicable facts depend on ABD-02/04/05. Recommendations are decimal-exact storage (never floating point for stock/cost) and explicit unit metadata; do not turn the earlier numeric(18,3) example into an approved fractional-stock rule. Negative receipts are not corrections. Posted quantities require an explicit correction workflow.

Reuse InventoryProduct and Supplier identities. A product may be bought from different suppliers; supplier belongs to the procurement line rather than a mandatory supplier field on the product. Use distinct lines when product, unit, supplier, price, currency, warranty or procurement reference differs. Never use supplier-name equality as a unique supplier key. Keep received specifications as historical facts so later catalogue edits do not rewrite procurement history.

Purchase date is a purchase fact, received_at is physical arrival and created_at is server recording time. An invoice/order/delivery reference is not a unique global ID or proof of a distinct delivery. Propose line purchase_reference varchar storage, separate from the existing header external_reference (arrival/document reference); exact applicability follows ABD-05. Multiple arrivals can share a purchase reference. Invoice/order entities are not introduced merely to store a reference. Currency and unit cost are paired facts; free or unknown cost must not both be encoded as zero. A later policy may require explicit completeness states/reasons; do not add placeholder suppliers or invented warranty periods.

**Partial deliveries:** propose a separate receipt for each arrival; a purchase reference links them for review. Record actual quantity only. Ordered/remaining quantities require a future purchase-order contract or explicit imported reference, not subtraction from an unverified number. Short/extra/damaged acceptance and tolerances require ABD-06. Approval of a purchase order is not automatically approval of registration.

**Duplicate prevention:** technical controls are a unique (receipt_id,line_number), one idempotency claim per logical command, source-system/record keys for imports, and a unique source-line/unit ordinal for eventual individual assets. Supplier/document/date/product similarities are duplicate warnings until their business reliability is established. A different request key can still describe the same delivery; do not claim network retry protection detects all physical duplicates. Repeated references across partial deliveries must remain possible.

**Registration timing:** R-01 immediate registration and R-10 approval are both confirmed. Recommend capturing the registration record immediately with approval as a separate state, withholding allocation until approval. A receipt header alone is not full asset registration under R-01/R-02. Pending status must not be confused with the physical Available/Damaged/Repair state. ABD-10 sets authority and provisional-use policy; the current system still has no operational registration endpoint.

## Individual assets and legacy identity

Use an automatically generated immutable internal UUID independently of visible numbering. Each future assets row represents one physical unit and references its receipt_line plus unit_ordinal; enforce unique(source_receipt_line_id,unit_ordinal). Individual received quantity must be integral and final asset count must equal the accepted registration quantity. Serialize registration under the source-line lock; a row CHECK alone cannot enforce aggregate counts, category mode or concurrent over-registration.

A visible asset ID is required, but neither LED format nor sequence/scope is specified. Recommend an immutable company-wide visible number after ABD-07 approval. If issuing-location scope is selected, use immutable issuing_location_id in the key and labels; current storage location is not a stable uniqueness scope. Use a unique index plus a database sequence or atomic scoped counter only after namespace/gap rules are settled; never MAX + 1. No starting value or example LED label is proposed here.

Keep serial capture/search distinct from internal identity. Preserve supplied serial spelling; normalize/reject duplicate serials only under ABD-08's agreed scope. SKU storage remains optional and non-unique in InventoryProduct. Category tracking is already a category attribute; a pooled delivery quantity does not bypass individual registration. Mixed tracking within categories requires an explicit extension, not an independent contradictory product flag (ABD-01).

Record observed condition, without silently defaulting to good/new. Retain the five R-02 statuses; do not add Lost, Consumed or Pending Approval as lifecycle statuses. Approval is separate. Damaged/uncertain arrival assessment is ABD-09; transitions and authority require future workflow design. Location means physical location, employee allocation means custody, and the PDF's company-owned scope does not specify multi-company ownership or borrowed assets.

Legacy import requires an approved source export, preserved source-system/row/batch keys and original labels, duplicate/unknown-data review and an accountable mapping decision (ABD-04/07/08/11). Existing LED labels are not evidence of the required future numbering format. Establish namespace reservations or a high-water value only after actual approved data is examined. No export was inspected in Task 8C. Opening receipts/stock must be explicitly identified as historical import, not fabricated new purchases; date, receiver and missing-data representation need the approved exception contract. Do not merge records merely because labels or serials match.

## Quantity lots and stock

Recommend an internal UUID lot for one homogeneous quantity receipt line. The line fixes product, procurement and unit; lot received_quantity must equal its registered source quantity. Keep the unit stable for historical interpretation. Unit conversion, precision and whole-count rules depend on ABD-02; pooled reusable versus consumable scope depends on ABD-12. Quantity category checks and lot/source equality need locks and reviewed database enforcement, not a subquery hidden in a CHECK.

A lot may span several physical locations: separate balances keyed by (lot_id,location_id,bucket) rather than changing lot provenance. Proposed availability/condition buckets require workflow review. Enforce quantity >= 0 and 0 <= reserved <= available-bucket quantity. Available-to-allocate = physical available-bucket quantity minus reserved; reservations remain a subset of stock, not additional quantity. No cross-unit sum should be presented as a count of assets.

Proposed append-only movements represent receiving, reservation/release, allocation, verified return, location transfer and approved correction/disposal. Only workflows implemented with their required approvals may create them. Same-lot transfer pairs conserve quantity; pending/unverified returns must not become available. Lot conservation is: received quantity + approved net corrections = company-held balances + outstanding custody + approved outflows. Consuming supplies or excusing an employee return is not approved by this equation.

Concurrent receiving locks the source line; stock changes lock affected balances in stable ID order and verify both local checks and transaction-level reconciliation. Journal, balance projection, business reference, audit and idempotent outcome must commit together. Use bounded deadlock/serialization retries with the same request key. Deferred reconciliation/trigger enforcement and direct-runtime privilege tests are future implementation work. Do not enable direct balance edits as a shortcut. Repeated registration cannot create a second lot for the same source line under the proposed one-line/one-lot design.

## Shared idempotency contract — engineering recommendation

This section refines the earlier schema-plan alternatives. It settles recommended mechanics without claiming a management decision about retry duration or privacy retention. Do not implement the model in Task 8C.

| Topic | Recommended contract |
|---|---|
| Key scope | Unique (authenticated local principal_id, versioned operation_scope, idempotency_key). Scope includes method, command family and target resource ID when relevant; never derive the principal from an untrusted actor field. Key is an opaque caller-generated token, nonblank and bounded by the existing proposed 200-character limit. No tenant field is assumed without a tenancy requirement. |
| Fingerprint | SHA-256 of deterministic canonical validated command content plus scope/schema version. Normalize object-key order, decimal/unit representations and date formats deliberately; keep ordered arrays ordered and distinguish meaningful null/absence. Include stable upload references/checksums where applicable, not file bytes or credentials. A change in canonicalization needs a versioned scope to avoid misreading old keys. |
| Changed payload | A committed key with a different fingerprint returns 409 and performs no business mutation. The same payload under a new key still needs business duplicate safeguards. |
| Concurrent request | Claim the unique key in the same transaction as the command. A competing insert waits within a bounded engineering-configured timeout. After the winner commits, load its result; if it rolls back, the next claimant may execute. On timeout return a safe retryable in-progress response using the same key. Use ON CONFLICT/savepoints so an expected uniqueness conflict does not leave the whole transaction aborted. Do not assume an uncommitted processing row is visible to a second connection. |
| Successful replay | Re-authenticate/re-authorize before every replay, then return the stored stable result/status and resource references without creating a second resource/audit event. A permission revocation overrides replay access. Replay the operation's result, not a newly executed command or necessarily the resource's current state. |
| Failure | Recommend success-only durable replay initially. Authentication/input failures create no claim; business/audit/constraint failures roll back the claim and all effects. No persisted FAILED response in the initial contract. A later corrected request should use a fresh key; a retry after a rollback may re-evaluate current business state. Server errors must not be cached as permanent responses. |
| Transaction boundary | PROCESSING exists only within the open command transaction; commit only SUCCEEDED with safe result metadata. Do not separately commit a lease/processing row or a failure cache. A durable unexplained PROCESSING row is an integrity incident requiring investigation, not automatic re-execution after a timer. |
| Response/privacy | Prefer status, stable resource IDs and a minimal non-sensitive confirmation over a full response-body copy. Exclude tokens, raw credentials, detailed employee/supplier data and unrestricted request payloads. A fingerprint is not encryption and may disclose low-entropy inputs if exposed; keep it internal and access-controlled. Sanitize log/error output. |
| Expiry | Keep expiry optional in the proposed schema; no invented duration or background deletion. ABD-13 sets the retry guarantee and confirmation retention. Never silently recycle an expired committed key: retain a minimal duplicate marker or reject older requests using a separately specified verifiable cutoff. Referential history markers must not be deleted while referenced. |

This recommendation supersedes the earlier optional persistent FAILED-response and automatic orphan-recovery ideas for the initial slice. The core request model now has a coherent offline contract (UUID PK, principal/scope/key unique, fingerprint, processing/succeeded status, minimal successful result and timestamps). Task 8D implemented this core offline without an expiry field. PostgreSQL migration and concurrency testing need separate authorization; management must approve retention before activation/cleanup. There is no need to ask management to choose SHA-256, SQL locking or database keys.

## Dependencies and next step

Task 8C implemented no additional model. Existing InventoryProduct and Receipt need no new business approval for their unchanged independent scope. Task 8D subsequently implemented the core idempotency model and pure helpers offline, with no expiry scheduler, failure cache, endpoint or operational enablement. Receipt lines require ABD-02/04/05 and a documented unit/receiver treatment from ABD-01/03; assets require numbering, serial, condition and approval decisions; lots require receipt lines and ABD-01/02/12. Stock journal/balances, history/provenance and allocation/return/repair/disposal require future workflow enforcement design.

Authentication and audit policies remain unchanged. Permanent audit grants are pending, employee registration stays disabled, and R-11 before/after value capture/redaction remains incomplete. No new business workflow can bypass these prerequisites by relying solely on an offline model.
