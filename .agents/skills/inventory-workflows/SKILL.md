# Inventory workflows

Use this skill for inventory lifecycle design, implementation, and review.

- Cover asset receiving and registration; employee onboarding, transfer, and exit; allocation and return; repairs; disposal; and approvals.
- Model valid state transitions, required actors, timestamps, evidence, and audit events for each workflow.
- Validate duplicate prevention, ownership, availability, authorization, and concurrent-operation rules at the service and persistence boundaries.
- Keep interruption, retry, failure, and rollback behavior explicit for multi-step operations.
- Distinguish confirmed company rules from proposed workflow behavior and unresolved decisions.
- Do not create approval chains, statuses, retention rules, or exception policies that are not supported by requirements or an explicit decision.
