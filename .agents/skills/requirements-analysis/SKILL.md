# Requirements analysis

Use this skill when extracting, clarifying, or maintaining the Inventory AIOS requirements.

- Read `docs/requirements/Inventory & Asset Management Automation System.pdf` when it is available; if the file is absent or named differently, report that fact rather than silently substituting a source.
- Maintain traceability across all 17 requirement sections, preserving section identifiers and linking each requirement to its source evidence.
- Label statements as confirmed requirements, proposals, assumptions, or unresolved decisions. Do not present proposals as approved policy.
- Record missing business decisions explicitly and identify the decision owner or required input when known.
- Do not invent company policies, workflows, permissions, approval rules, or acceptance criteria.
- Keep analysis artifacts concise, reviewable, and synchronized with the source PDF.
